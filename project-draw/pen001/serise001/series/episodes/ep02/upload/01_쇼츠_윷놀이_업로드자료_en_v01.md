## 설명
How did people in Joseon-era Korea spend a day off?
Four people sit around a low board. Today's star is yutnori.

Instead of dice, players throw four wooden sticks and move their pieces around the board, choosing routes and racing to bring every piece home. A lucky throw helps, but a smart choice matters too. Traditionally played around the Lunar New Year, the game brings families and neighbors together.

For 19th-century visitors to Korea, pictures like this were a window onto everyday life in an unfamiliar country.
