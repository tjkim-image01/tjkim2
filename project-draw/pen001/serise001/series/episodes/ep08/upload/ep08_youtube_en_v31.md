## 제목
A quiet contest

## 설명
A chess player might recognize this concentration. But these lettered pieces belong to janggi, Korean chess. Each kind of piece moves by its own rules. The players must read the position and anticipate another move. Notice the lowered heads and the hands reaching toward the board. To a visitor, both the familiar contest and the unfamiliar pieces could be intriguing. You need not read every character to recognize the waiting and the thought. The picture preserves concentration rather than the final result. The work is attributed to Uncho. From the TJ Kim Collection, a quiet game lets strangers share a familiar human moment.
