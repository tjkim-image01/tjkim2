## 설명
Before electric irons, how did people make a garment smooth?
Two women beat cloth on a low stone in a Korean practice called dadeumijil. Washed and starched fabric was pounded to smooth its surface.

Follow the wooden beaters and the different angles of their arms. This genre painting brings the often unseen work behind a clean white garment into view.
