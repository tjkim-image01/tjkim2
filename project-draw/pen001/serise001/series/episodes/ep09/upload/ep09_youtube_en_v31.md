Food takes teamwork

## 설명
A good meal can depend on a well timed movement. Here, cooked grain is being pounded to make Korean rice cake. One worker brings a wooden mallet down while another turns and shapes the mass. Strength matters, but so does knowing when the other hand will move. Trace the bent back, the lifted arms, and the long handles. Another person and a passing dog make this a lived in workplace. A visitor might wonder about both the food and the tools used to make it. Look beyond the finished dish to the skill of working together. The painting is attributed to Uncho. From the TJ Kim Collection, the smallest pause between hands becomes part of the story.
