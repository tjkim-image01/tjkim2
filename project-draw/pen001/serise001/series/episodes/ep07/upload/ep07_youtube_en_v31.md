## 설명
A low table meets people seated on the floor.
This small Korean table is called a soban. It could hold a meal and carry dishes into a room. Moved away after use, it suited a room serving more than one purpose.

Look at the height of the bowls, the hands and the seated bodies. This is one chosen scene rather than a picture of every household. A small piece of furniture helps us read the shape of a day.
