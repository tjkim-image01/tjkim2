## en 설명
History, read through objects — TJ Kim Collection.

What stories hide inside an old postcard, a faded map, or a well-thumbed book?

From the TJ Kim Collection, we bring out Korean postcards, rare books, antique maps, prints and archival records, and tell the stories of the eras they passed through and the everyday lives of the people who held them — simply and vividly.

From quick history Shorts to in-depth looks at the original objects,
join us in finding moments in old things that resemble our own lives today.
